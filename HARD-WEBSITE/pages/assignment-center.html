01 / INTRODUCTION

Learning must
be put into practice.

The Assignment Center serves as an informational space outlining the academic responsibilities and learning activities within H.A.R.D.

Through structured assignments, trainees are encouraged to develop critical thinking, analytical ability, communication, and responsible reasoning.

02 / ASSIGNMENT CATEGORIES

Different tasks.
One standard of excellence.

01
Ⅰ

Analytical Tasks

Activities that encourage trainees to examine information, identify issues, and construct logical interpretations.

02
Ⅱ

Argumentative Tasks

Exercises focused on developing arguments, presenting viewpoints, and supporting claims with relevant reasoning.

03
Ⅲ

Collaborative Tasks

Activities designed to strengthen teamwork, discussion, coordination, and collective problem-solving.

04
Ⅳ

Reflective Tasks

Assignments that invite trainees to evaluate their learning process, contributions, and personal development.

03 / GENERAL GUIDELINES

Before you begin.

Every assignment is an opportunity to demonstrate discipline, integrity, and intellectual responsibility.

01

Understand the Instructions

Read and understand all requirements before beginning an assignment.

02

Maintain Academic Integrity

Submit original work and acknowledge relevant sources when necessary.

03

Respect the Deadline

Complete and submit assignments according to the schedule established by the organizers.

04

Follow Submission Procedures

Use the external submission method and instructions officially provided by the organizers.

04 / SUBMISSION INFORMATION

The process
continues beyond
this page.

Assignment distribution, deadlines, and submission instructions will be communicated through the official channels designated by the H.A.R.D. organizers.

This website serves as an informational reference. It does not provide an assignment upload or grading system.
