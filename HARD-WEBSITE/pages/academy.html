THE PURPOSE

LEARNING THROUGH REASON

Beyond knowledge.
Toward understanding.

H.A.R.D. Academy is the learning and development space of the institution. It introduces trainees to the principles, knowledge, and practices that support their development throughout the training process.

Learning is not limited to receiving information. It also involves examining ideas, constructing arguments, and understanding the responsibility that comes with every judgment.
