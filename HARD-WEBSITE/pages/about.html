THE BEGINNING OF A LEGACY

H.A.R.D. was established in 2025 as a space dedicated to the development of rational thinking, structured argumentation, and responsible judgment.

Through its activities and learning process, H.A.R.D. seeks to create an environment where every perspective can be examined and every argument can be understood through reason.
