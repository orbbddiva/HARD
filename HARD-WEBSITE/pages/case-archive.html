ARCHIVAL RULE / 01

Only completed cases that have received final publication approval may be preserved in the Case Archive.

THE ARCHIVAL PROCEDURE

Before a case
becomes history.

Every record must pass through a clear sequence before entering the archive.

01

Active Case

The case remains under institutional examination and activity.

02

Resolution

The case reaches its formal conclusion.

03

Publication Review

The record is reviewed for accuracy, confidentiality, and publication suitability.

04

Final Approval

The designated authority grants final permission for archival publication.

05

Case Archive

The approved record becomes part of the institution's preserved history.
